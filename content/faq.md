# PS Plus FAQ

Answers to the questions people ask most about PlayStation Plus. For what each tier includes and when games arrive, see [About PS Plus](/about).

## The basics

### What's the difference between Essential, Extra and Premium?

Essential gives you online multiplayer, cloud saves, member discounts and a few monthly games to claim. Extra adds the Game Catalog, a library of hundreds of PS4 and PS5 games you can download. Premium adds the Classics Catalog of older PlayStation games, time-limited game trials of new releases and cloud streaming. Each tier includes everything below it. ([PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-in/support/subscriptions/playstation-plus-support/))

### How much does it cost?

In the US, a month costs $10.99 for Essential, $16.99 for Extra and $19.99 for Premium for new members, after Sony raised the monthly and three-month prices in May 2026. Twelve-month plans weren't affected and work out cheaper. Prices vary by country. ([gagadget](https://gagadget.com/en/711625-sony-raises-ps-plus-prices-on-all-tiers-for-new-subscribers/))

### What is PS Plus Deluxe?

It's the top tier in countries without cloud streaming. It has the Classics Catalog and game trials but no streaming, which also means no PS3 games. ([The Outerhaven](https://www.theouterhaven.net/2022/03/ps-now-and-ps-plus-merged-to-create-new-three-tier-subscription-service))

### Do I need PS Plus to play online?

For most games you buy, yes. Most free-to-play games such as Fortnite, Apex Legends and Call of Duty: Warzone don't need it. ([PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-bh/ps-plus/))

### Is PS Plus still adding PS4 games?

Only now and then. Sony says that from January 2026, PS4 games will be added only intermittently, so most new arrivals are PS5 games. ([PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/psplus))

### What happened to PlayStation Now?

It was folded into PS Plus in June 2022. Its streaming became part of Premium, and PS Now subscribers were moved to Premium at no extra cost. ([PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2022/03/29/all-new-playstation-plus-launches-in-june-with-700-games-and-more-value-than-ever/comment-page-5))

## Monthly games

### When are the monthly games announced?

Usually on the last Wednesday before the first Tuesday of the month, on the PlayStation Blog. They become available that Tuesday. Sony occasionally moves the announcement, as it did in July 2026 when it came on 1 July. ([OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/news/33400/playstation-plus-july-2026-free-games-lineup-comes-with-a-unique-catch))

### How long do I have to claim them?

Only during their month, until the next month's games arrive on the first Tuesday. After that they can't be added to your library. ([PSPrices](https://psprices.com/region-tr/ps-plus-monthly-alias/))

### Do I need to download a monthly game to keep it?

No. Adding it to your library from the PlayStation Store or the PS Plus hub is enough. You can download it whenever you like, even years later.

### Do I keep the monthly games if I cancel?

They stay in your library, but you can only play them while you're subscribed. If you rejoin later, at any tier, they work again. ([PSPrices](https://psprices.com/region-us/collection/ps-plus-monthly?platform=PS4))

### Can I claim a PS5 monthly game if I only have a PS4?

Usually, yes. Claiming it on the PlayStation website or app adds it to your library, ready for when you get a PS5.

### What was the PS Plus Collection?

A set of 19 big PS4 games that PS5 owners could claim, from the PS5's launch in 2020 until 9 May 2023. Anyone who claimed them before then can still play them while subscribed. ([Video Games Chronicle](https://videogameschronicle.com/news/the-ps-plus-collection-library-shuts-down-today))

### Where can I see every monthly game ever given?

On [every PS Plus monthly game](/lists/ps-plus-monthly-games-history), which lists the PS4 and PS5 lineups month by month since November 2013.

## Game Catalog and Classics

### When do new catalogue games arrive?

Sony announces them around the middle of the month, usually on a Wednesday, and they generally arrive the following Tuesday, often the third Tuesday of the month. ([Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2022/07/when-will-new-ps-plus-extra-ps-plus-premium-games-be-announced))

### Why did a game arrive on a different day for me?

Since June 2026, Sony has spread each month's catalogue additions over several weeks in the US, UK and Japan. Everywhere else still gets them on a single day. In November 2026, for instance, US and UK members got games on 11, 17 and 24 November, while most other countries got nearly all of them on the 17th. ([Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/06/absolutely-terrible-idea-nobody-likes-sonys-new-ps-plus-experiment), [Fandomwire](https://fandomwire.com/playstation-reveals-ps-plus-game-catalog-november-2026-all-games/))

### How do I know when a game is leaving?

Sony says leaving dates are announced ahead of time. On your console, look for the Last Chance to Play section in the PS Plus hub; on this site, check [Leaving soon](/lists/leaving-ps-plus). ([PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-bh/ps-plus/))

### What happens to my save if a game leaves?

Your save stays on your console and in cloud storage. If you buy the game, or it comes back to PS Plus, you can pick up where you left off.

### Can I play a catalogue game from a disc I own?

No. A catalogue game is a separate digital licence; your disc plays as normal on its own, but it isn't linked to PS Plus.

### Is the catalogue the same everywhere?

No. Availability varies by country, so a game listed for one region may not be in yours. The PS Plus hub on your console shows what's available where you are. ([PlayStation](https://playstation.com/en-in/ps-plus/games))

### Can I play PS3 games?

Only with Premium, and only by cloud streaming. They can't be downloaded. ([PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-gb/ps-plus/games/))

## Streaming, PC and sharing

### Can I stream games instead of downloading them?

With Premium, yes. You can stream PS4 games from the Game Catalog and games from the Classics Catalog to a PS4 or PS5, and some PS5 games to a PS5 or PlayStation Portal. Sony asks for at least 5 Mbps, or 15 Mbps for 1080p. ([PlayStation](https://playstation.com/ps-plus/getting-started), [PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-gb/ps-plus/games/))

### Can I play PS Plus games on a PC?

Not for much longer. Premium members could stream PS4 catalogue games and selected classics through Sony's PC app, but Sony is retiring the app on 10 December 2026, saying fewer than 0.4% of eligible Premium members used it. After that there's no official way to stream PS Plus games to a PC. Streaming to PS5, PS4 and PlayStation Portal carries on. ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/sony-shuts-down-ps-plus-pc-app/), [Keengamer](https://www.keengamer.com/articles/news/sony-retires-the-playstation-plus-pc-app-on-december-10/))

### Can other people on my console use my PS Plus?

Yes. On a PS5, turn on Console Sharing and Offline Play; on a PS4, make it your primary console. Other accounts on that console can then play your PS Plus games and play online. ([PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-in/support/subscriptions/playstation-plus-support/))

### Does my membership carry over from PS4 to PS5?

Yes. PS Plus belongs to your account, not your console, so just sign in on the new machine. ([PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-in/support/subscriptions/playstation-plus-support/))

### Can PS4 catalogue games be played on PS5?

Yes. PS4 games can be downloaded to either console, but PS5 games only run on a PS5. ([PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-bh/ps-plus/games))

## Billing

### Can I upgrade my tier partway through a membership?

Yes. Sony works out the upgrade cost from the time you have left on your current plan, so you don't lose what you've paid for. Upgrade deals also turn up during PlayStation sales. ([PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/ps-now/))

### Can I stack memberships?

You can buy a new membership at any time and it will start when your current one ends. ([PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-gb/ps-plus/games/))

### Will I pay the new price if I'm already a member?

Sony's May 2026 increase applied to new members. Existing members kept their rate, but anyone who lets their membership lapse and rejoins pays the new price. ([Push Square](https://pushsquare.com/news/2026/05/ps-plus-price-increase-announced-by-sony-for-new-members))

### Does PS Plus renew automatically?

Yes. Memberships continue until you cancel, which you can do from your account settings on the console or the PlayStation website. If you cancel, you keep your benefits until the end of the period you've paid for. ([PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/psplus))

### Is there a free trial?

Sometimes. Sony offers trials of Extra, Deluxe or Premium in some countries, for people who aren't currently members. ([PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-za/ps-plus/games))
