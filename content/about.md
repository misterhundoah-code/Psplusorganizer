# About PS Plus and Wall of PS Plus

## What is PS Plus?

PlayStation Plus is Sony's subscription for PS4 and PS5. At its simplest it unlocks online multiplayer and gives you a few games to claim each month. The higher tiers add a rotating library of hundreds of PS4 and PS5 games you can download and play for as long as you subscribe, much like Netflix for games, and the top tier adds classic games from older PlayStations, game trials and cloud streaming. Games come and go every month, which is exactly why this site exists.

## A short history

Today's PS Plus is two services stitched together: the original PS Plus, which gave subscribers free games, and PlayStation Now, Sony's game-streaming service. They merged in 2022.

| When | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| June 2010 | PS Plus launches on PS3 on 29 June, as an optional extra on top of the free PlayStation Network, with discounts, early demos and some free games. ([Sony](https://sonyinteractive.com/en/?p=3005618)) |
| June 2012 | The Instant Game Collection arrives, the start of free monthly games for subscribers. ([PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2013/06/28/celebrating-1-year-of-the-playstation-plus-instant-game-collection/comment-page-2)) |
| November 2013 | The PS4 launches, and PS Plus becomes required for most online multiplayer. |
| January 2014 | Sony announces PlayStation Now at CES, a streaming service built on technology from Gaikai. Its open beta starts on PS4 in the US and Canada that July. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PlayStation_Now)) |
| 2014 to 2022 | PS Now grows to stream PS3 games and later lets you download PS2 and PS4 games too, while PS Plus keeps giving monthly games. |
| November 2020 | The PS5 launches with the PS Plus Collection, a set of big PS4 games for PS5 owners to claim. |
| June 2022 | PS Plus and PS Now merge into one service with three tiers: Essential, Extra and Premium. PS Now subscribers move to Premium, and the new PS Plus reaches the Americas on 13 June. ([PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2022/03/29/all-new-playstation-plus-launches-in-june-with-700-games-and-more-value-than-ever/comment-page-14)) |
| May 2023 | The PS Plus Collection is retired on 9 May. Games already claimed stay playable while you subscribe. ([Video Games Chronicle](https://videogameschronicle.com/news/the-ps-plus-collection-library-shuts-down-today)) |
| January 2026 | Sony says new PS4 games will be added to the catalogue only now and then from here on, with the focus on PS5. ([PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/psplus)) |
| June 2026 | Sony starts staggering Game Catalog releases across several weeks in the US, UK and Japan, while the rest of the world gets each month's games on one day. ([Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/06/absolutely-terrible-idea-nobody-likes-sonys-new-ps-plus-experiment)) |

## The tiers

Each tier includes everything in the one below it.

| | Essential | Extra | Premium |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Online multiplayer, cloud saves, Share Play, member discounts | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Monthly games to claim | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Game Catalog: hundreds of PS4 and PS5 games to download | | Yes | Yes |
| Ubisoft+ Classics | | Yes | Yes |
| Classics Catalog: PS1, PS2 and PSP games, plus PS3 games by streaming | | | Yes |
| Game trials of new releases | | | Yes |
| Cloud streaming to PS5, PS4 and PlayStation Portal (Sony is closing the PC app on 10 December 2026) | | | Yes |
| US price, one month (from May 2026) | $10.99 | $16.99 | $19.99 |

In countries without cloud streaming, the top tier is called Deluxe instead of Premium: it has the Classics Catalog and game trials but no streaming and no PS3 games. Prices change from time to time and differ by country; the one-month US prices above are from Sony's May 2026 increase for new members. ([gagadget](https://gagadget.com/en/711625-sony-raises-ps-plus-prices-on-all-tiers-for-new-subscribers/), [PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-in/support/subscriptions/playstation-plus-support/))

## The PS Plus calendar

Sony runs PS Plus on a steady monthly rhythm, all announced on the PlayStation Blog.

| What | Announced | Arrives | Goes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly games (every tier) | The last Wednesday before the first Tuesday of the month | The first Tuesday of the month | Claimable until the next month's games arrive; once claimed, yours while you subscribe |
| Game Catalog and Classics (Extra and Premium) | A Wednesday in the middle of the month | Usually the third Tuesday. In the US, UK and Japan, Sony now spreads them over a few weeks | Stay for months or years, until Sony removes them |
| Games leaving the catalogue | Shown in the "Last Chance to Play" section of the PS Plus hub, usually about a month ahead | | On the catalogue update day, usually the third Tuesday |

November 2026 is a good example of the new staggered catalogue: the lineup was announced on 10 November, and in the US and UK the games arrived on 11, 17 and 24 November, while most other countries got nearly all of them on 17 November. ([Fandomwire](https://fandomwire.com/playstation-reveals-ps-plus-game-catalog-november-2026-all-games/), [Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2022/07/when-will-new-ps-plus-extra-ps-plus-premium-games-be-announced))

The Wall follows all of this for you: the This month panel shows the current monthly games with their claim-by date, the games about to leave and what Sony has announced is coming, and you can add any of those dates to your calendar.

## How to use the Wall

Wall of PS Plus shows every game in the PS Plus Game Catalog, the Classics Catalog and the monthly games, on one wall you can sort and filter. Sony's own browser makes it hard to answer simple questions like "what's a short co-op game I haven't played?", so this site tries to make that easy.

**Find something to play.** Use the filters to narrow the wall by PS Plus tier, style, length, number of players, platform, camera perspective, original system and age rating, then sort by Metascore, completion time, release year, title or a priority score tuned to your own ratings. Search covers titles, styles and your own notes. Each game shows its Metascore and its HowLongToBeat time, so you can see at a glance how good it is and how long it will take.

**Keep track of what you've played.** Rate games out of five, note how far you got, and record why you bounced off the ones you dropped. Mark the monthly games you claimed so the wall knows which ones you can still play. Once you've rated a few, Your taste profile shows the styles you favour, the ones that miss, and how your ratings compare with the critics. Your marks stay in your browser unless you create an account, which keeps an encrypted copy that only your password can unlock.

**Bring in your PlayStation library.** If you choose to, you can import the games you've bought or claimed from your PlayStation account, so the wall can hide or flag them. This is entirely optional and only happens when you ask for it.

**Keep an eye on the calendar.** The This month panel shows the current monthly games, what's leaving the catalogue soon and what's on the way. You can download a calendar reminder for the last day to claim a monthly game or for the day a new game arrives.

**Browse the lists.** The bar at the top links to [ready-made lists](/lists/) such as the best of Extra, co-op games, short games and horror, each with hand-picked recommendations, to [every monthly game since 2013](/lists/ps-plus-monthly-games-history), and to [Predictions](/lists/coming-to-ps-plus), which estimates which games are likely to come to PS Plus next.
