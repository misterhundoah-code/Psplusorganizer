Questions

How do I sort PS Plus Extra games by Metacritic score or length?

Choose Metacritic score or Completion time under Sort, and narrow it down with the Length, Platform, Players and style filters. Your choices are remembered next time.

Which games are leaving PS Plus this month?

Sony has confirmed 4 games leaving the Game Catalogue on 15 December 2026: Dark Rose Valkyrie, Fade to Silence, Granblue Fantasy: Relink, Somerville.

Do I need to sign in with my PSN account?

No. The site never asks for your PlayStation account. Your marks and ratings are saved in your browser. A free, optional account (a made-up username and a password, no email) syncs them between devices, encrypted so only your password unlocks them.

Does it show the catalogue for my country?

Yes. Pick your country at the top of the filters and the wall shows that country's Game Catalogue, Classics and age ratings, from Sony's own lists.

Is this site made by Sony or does it use AI?

It's an independent fan-made tool, not affiliated with Sony. It was built with the help of Claude, Anthropic's AI model; no AI runs while you use it, and your data is never sent to an AI service. The AI disclosure link at the top has the details.

Where the numbers come from. Game Catalogue: Sony's PlayStation Plus feed (October 2026 snapshot), updated by hand with the October and November 2026 additions and removals. Which games show for your country comes from Sony's own Game Catalogue and Classics lists for that country, checked twice a week; Sony publishes none for Brazil, so Brazil uses the Latin American list. Monthly games: every PS4 and PS5 monthly game in North America, from the November 2013 PS4 launch to October 2022 as listed by the PlayStation Fandom wiki, and the Essential line-ups from November 2022 to November 2026 as reported by Game Rant, Insider Gaming and Beebom. PS3 and PS Vita monthly games aren't included. A game offered twice shows its most recent month. Many older monthly games no longer have Metascores or completion times here, and a few have been delisted from the store. Metacritic: Metascores collected July 2026, with about 150 gaps checked on metacritic.com on 16 November 2026. Completion times: HowLongToBeat averages collected March 2026.
How "Priority for me" works. It starts from a game's Metascore (68 when there is none) and moves it up or down by as much as about 20 points according to your taste. Taste comes from the styles and lengths of the games you have marked: a 5-star rating counts strongly for those styles, 3 stars is neutral, 1 star and Bounced count against, and Want and Not interested count a little. The small number beside the score is how far your taste moved it. It switches on once you have rated or bounced off three games, and it is a rough guide based on broad style tags, so treat it as a nudge.
Classics. Sony's Premium Classics list for your country: PS1, PS2 and PSP games, PS4 remasters and two arcade fighters. PS3 streaming. Sony doesn't publish a list of the PS3 games Premium streams, so the 328 here come from Thumbsticks' list of the Classics Catalog, cross-checked against PlayStation Universe's (19 November 2026). That's the US and Canadian line-up: 67 of them aren't offered elsewhere and are hidden outside the US and Canada, and countries whose top tier is Deluxe rather than Premium (such as Brazil, Mexico and Australia) get no PS3 streaming at all. The line-up changes from time to time, so check the console before you count on one. Metacritic reviewed few PS1-era games, so many Classics have no Metascore, and some scores are for another platform's release. Times are HowLongToBeat averages for the original release.
Games you add. A game you add carries only what you enter, or what its PlayStation Store page shows if you paste the link (title, platforms, genres and release year). Added games have no Metascore, are never recommended back to you, and are saved with your marks.
Notes, dates and AI picks. Marking a game Now playing or Played records the date. Notes are yours and are saved with your marks. The Copy prompt button only puts text on your clipboard: what you paste, and where, is up to you, and any picks you get back are suggestions, not facts.
Accounts and privacy. You can use this site without an account; your marks then stay in this browser. An account only needs a made-up username and a password: no email address, no name. Your marks are encrypted in your browser with a key made from your password before they are sent, so the server keeps only an unreadable copy. That also means nobody can reset a forgotten password, so download a backup now and then. The site sets no cookies and loads no trackers. Visits are counted anonymously with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and doesn't identify you. See the Privacy Policy for the details.
Keeping it current. The game lists live in the site's data files and are updated by hand or by a scheduled job; the date in the header shows the last update. Games that have left the catalogue disappear from Browse but stay in your tabs if you had marked them.
Progress and bounce reasons. On a game you are playing you can record how far in you are and your hours; on a game you bounced off you can say why. Reasons about the kind of game (combat, story, controls, repetition, not your kind of game) count against its styles in your taste profile; "too long" counts against that length; "too hard" or "performance" alone counts only lightly.
Perspective and synopses. The synopses were written by Claude from general knowledge of each game, not taken from the store, and have not been checked one by one. Perspective comes from HowLongToBeat where it lists one and was filled in by hand for the rest; "Multiple views" covers racing, sports and other games where you switch cameras.
Things to know. Artwork is from the PlayStation Store and belongs to Sony Interactive Entertainment and the games' publishers; it is stored with this site, so showing it doesn't contact Sony. Games without artwork get a coloured tile with their initials. The country you pick under Your PS Plus decides which Game Catalogue and Classics games show and which store the links open. Games new to your country's list can take a few days to appear here. Metacritic now shows one Metascore per game, usually for its lead platform, so a few scores reflect the PC or Switch release. Special editions use the base game's score and playtime when they have none of their own. "tbd" means Metacritic has too few reviews to give a score. A time shown with ≈ has no main-story figure, so it uses the all-play-styles average; a time in grey comes from fewer than 10 player reports. The catalogue differs slightly by country and changes monthly, so check the PlayStation Store before you plan around a game.

Import from your PlayStation account

Mark the games you've played and tag the games you own, straight from your PlayStation account. It takes three steps, and you only need step 1 once.

  1. Add the bookmark. Drag this button to your browser's bookmarks bar: Copy my PlayStation library On a phone or if dragging doesn't work: , bookmark any page, then edit that bookmark and paste the code in as its address.
  2. Run it on Sony's site. Open library.playstation.com, sign in if asked, then click the bookmark (on a phone, type its name in the address bar and tap it). It copies your list.
  3. Paste it here.

The bookmark only talks to Sony, using the sign-in you already have there: it never sees your password, and its code is public. Your list is matched here in your browser and isn't sent anywhere, except in your encrypted account if you use one. It relies on how Sony's site works today, so it may stop working if Sony changes it.

AI disclosure

I'm a PS Plus subscriber who wanted a better way to work through the catalogue, not a professional developer. I built this site with Claude, Anthropic's AI model. Claude wrote most of the code; I decided what the site should do, tested it and chose what went in.

What that means for you

  • No AI runs while you use the site. Nothing you do here is sent to Claude, Anthropic or any other AI service.
  • Most game information comes from published sources: availability and age ratings from Sony's PS Plus lists, scores from Metacritic, playtimes from HowLongToBeat, and the longer descriptions, credits and crossplay from Wikipedia and Wikidata.
  • Some was written with Claude's help: the one-line synopses, the camera view (first-person, top-down and so on) and the style tags were drafted with Claude when the site started and haven't all been checked by hand. If one is off, please report it.
  • The "why you might like it" notes are simple rules based on your own ratings, not AI.
  • Your marks stay in your browser unless you create an account, which stores an encrypted copy only your password can unlock, or opt in to community scores, which are shared anonymously and shown only as totals. The Privacy Policy has the details.
  • The code is public on GitHub, and the site is built straight from it, so anyone can check how it works.
