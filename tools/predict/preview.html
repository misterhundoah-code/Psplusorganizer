Likely to join PS Plus next

Every PS4 and PS5 game that isn't on PS Plus yet, ranked by how likely it is to join in the next three months. The odds come from a model trained on every lineup Sony has announced since 2022, so treat this as a watchlist rather than a promise.

    How it works

    For each month since PS Plus Extra launched (and since 2019 for the monthly games), every PS4 and PS5 game that could have been added is one example, and the games Sony actually added are the hits. The model learns how much each of these signals mattered. The bars show how strongly each one pushes a game up (green) or down (red).

    Rules and limits
    • The year's biggest launches are held back: nothing anywhere near as big as GTA VI has ever joined in its first year, so those wait until the year is up.
    • Free-to-play games are left out, since they don't need PS Plus.
    • Popularity is how often the game's Wikipedia article was read in the last 60 days. It's a good guide to interest today, but older games' launch-time buzz isn't captured.
    • Deals between Sony and publishers are invisible to the model, so surprise launch-day indies will always catch it out.
    • Games without a Wikipedia or Wikidata entry are missing or know less about themselves.

    Sources: Wikidata and Wikipedia (CC BY-SA) for games, publishers, release dates and page views; the PlayStation Blog for past lineups.
